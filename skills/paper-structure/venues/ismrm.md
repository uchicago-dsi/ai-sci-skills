# ISMRM standard abstract (2027)

**Verified:** 2026-10-17 against https://www.ismrm.org/27m/call/standard/.
**Register:** `journal-imrad`

Scientific meeting abstract for MRI researchers. State substantive findings rather than prospective analyses.

## Section skeleton

Suggested body: INTRODUCTION, METHODS, RESULTS, DISCUSSION, CONCLUSION, REFERENCES. The venue allows justified deviations. Separate synopsis has Motivation, Goal(s), Approach, Results. Impact is separate.

## Limits

Title: 125 characters. Synopsis: 100 words combined. Impact: 40 words. Body: 750 words excluding references. Up to five figures, each caption at most 500 characters. One additional captionless preview figure, legible on a smartphone. Equation word-count treatment is not specified: keep a conservative source-token count and verify in the actual portal rather than claiming equations are free. Do not assume tables provide extra figure slots.

## Abstract

Use direct scientific language; the official page does not impose passive voice or ban equations. Give counts, units and support for numerical claims. Keep synopsis labels separate and visible.

## References

Numerical superscript Arabic citations, references in order of first citation. Put grant funding, code, data and supporting links only in Acknowledgements, which are not shared with reviewers.

## Required apparatus

The standard page lists optional institutional/consortium logo and separate acknowledgements. Check the actual submission form for author, ethics, disclosure and approval requirements; do not infer that this page settles them.

## Submission mechanics

Official instructions: https://www.ismrm.org/27m/call/standard/. Upload figures separately with captions and preview separately without a caption. Verify image format, file-size, aspect ratio and submission-system counting in the current portal before external submission.

## Review model

The page does not establish the anonymity policy. It evaluates guideline adherence, originality, substantive results, appropriate statistics, image quality and impact. Check current full submission instructions before submitting.
